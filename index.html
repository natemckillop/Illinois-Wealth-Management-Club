Why We Exist

Financial literacy shouldn't wait until after graduation

Most students leave campus having never built a budget, read a credit report, or placed a real trade. We exist to close that gap — teaching Illini the principles that matter long before the stakes get high.

  • Investing fundamentals, from first principles to portfolio strategy
  • Credit cards and credit-building, explained without the jargon
  • Practical budgeting for a real student income
  • Strategic investment timing and market awareness

The Micro-Fund

Theory only goes so far — so we manage a real portfolio together

Members and officers move beyond the classroom with a student-managed micro-fund, built to give aspiring investors practical, hands-on stock market experience alongside their peers.

What We Cover

A curriculum built for real life, not just exams

Every semester of programming is anchored around four pillars of personal finance.

Investing Fundamentals

Stocks, bonds, funds, and the principles behind every sound investment decision.

Credit & Credit Cards

How credit actually works, and how to use it to your advantage — not the bank's.

Budgeting

Practical frameworks for managing a student income — and building habits that last.

Strategic Timing

Reading market conditions and understanding when — and why — to act.

Leadership

Run by students, for students

A seven-person officer team plans every meeting, manages the fund, and keeps the club running.
